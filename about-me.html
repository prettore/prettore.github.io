About Me

Short Bio

Paulo H. L. Rettore is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil. His current work focuses on intelligent environments (transportation systems, smart cities, smart farms, and smart homes), heterogeneous data fusion, data mining, applied AI, and resilient systems and networks.

He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Integrated Faculty of Pedro Leopoldo (Brazil) in 2009. During this time, he worked for four years in IT support and one year in system development. He earned his M.Sc. in Computer Science from the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) in 2012, where he developed research on hardware accelerators using high-level programming languages (C to FPGA).

He served as an Assistant Professor at the State University of Minas Gerais (UEMG) – Frutal Campus (2012) and at the Federal Institute of São Paulo (IFSP) – São Carlos Campus (2013). He obtained his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) in 2019, with research focused on intelligent transportation systems, heterogeneous data fusion, data mining, and machine learning.

After completing his Ph.D., he joined the Fraunhofer Institute for Communication, Information Processing and Ergonomics (FKIE) in Bonn, Germany, as a Research Scientist. At Fraunhofer FKIE, he contributed to research proposal writing, the development of research activities, scientific publications, and the supervision of master's students from the University of Bonn’s Institute of Computer Science.
